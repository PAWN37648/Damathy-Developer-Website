Privacy Policy for Damathy

Effective Date: October 20, 2026 | Last Updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Damathy ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your information when you install and play our Android mobile application. We are committed to maintaining high privacy standards and transparency.

1. Overview & Core Architecture

Damathy is an educational mathematics board game implementing Philippine Damath (Mathematical Checkers). The application is built client-side: all game logic, neural network evaluation (NNUE), bot artificial intelligence, tactical puzzle generation, and match histories are calculated and stored locally on your device.

We do not maintain private backend servers that collect, store, or sell your personal identifiable information. You do not need to register an account, log in, or provide your real name, email address, or phone number to access the game.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To ensure seamless gameplay across app sessions without requiring a remote database, Damathy stores the following data locally on your device via HTML5 Local Storage:

  • Game Preferences: Board orientation, piece rotation, sound/audio settings, animation states, and interface language.
  • Gameplay Records: Move history trees (PGN), captured piece logs, scores, and bot completion crown records.
  • Puzzle Progress & Quotas: Solved puzzle indices, daily bonus counters, and ad cooldown timestamps.

This information is confined to your device's native app sandbox and is never uploaded to our servers.

5. European Economic Area (EEA), UK & Swiss Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you reside in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you are entitled to specific legal rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR:

  • Legal Basis for Processing: We rely on our legitimate interests to deliver app functionality and maintain security, and your explicit consent for serving personalized advertisements.
  • User Consent Management: We utilize Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent SDK. When launching the app, users in applicable jurisdictions can choose to consent or refuse personalized ad targeting.
  • Your Rights: You have the right to access, rectify, restrict, or request erasure of your data, or object to processing. Because we do not store personal profiles on private servers, clearing your device's app storage immediately erases your local data.

6. United States State Privacy Rights (California CCPA/CPRA, Virginia, Colorado)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), California residents have specific privacy rights:

  • "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information": We do not sell your personal data for monetary consideration. However, sharing advertising identifiers with ad networks for cross-context behavioral advertising may be considered "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under US state privacy laws.
  • Opt-Out Mechanism: You can opt out of personalized / targeted advertising at any time by configuring your Android device settings:
    Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Damathy is an educational game designed for students, teachers, and math enthusiasts of all ages. We take children's privacy seriously and comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).
  • In accordance with Google Play's Families Policy, ad requests served to child-directed users are configured with tagForChildDirectedTreatment and tagForUnderAgeOfConsent, restricting ad networks to contextual, non-personalized advertising only.
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has provided personally identifiable information to a third party through the app, please contact us immediately so we can assist in removing it.

8. Data Retention & Deletion Instructions

  • Local Data: All local game records and preferences remain on your device indefinitely until you choose to delete them. You can delete all locally stored game data at any time by going to:
    Android Settings → Apps → Damathy → Storage → Clear Data / Clear Cache (or by uninstalling the application).
  • Ad Network Data: Data collected by Google AdMob is retained in accordance with Google's published data retention periods (typically between 9 and 14 months for pseudonymized ad measurement logs).

9. Security Measures

We protect your information through device-level sandbox isolation and require all integrated third-party SDKs to communicate exclusively over secure Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.3) encrypted connections.
